WHAT IS AN AXOLOTL?

Not a fish. Not a lizard. Something completely unique.

The axolotl (Ambystoma mexicanum) is a fascinating amphibian native to Mexico. Unlike most salamanders, it remains aquatic throughout its life, with feathery external gills and a calm, curious nature.

FAQs

Everything you need to know about axolotls and our services.

At Summer Axolotl Home, an axolotl is a rare neotenic salamander native to the ancient lakes of Mexico. Unlike most amphibians, it keeps its feathery external gills and aquatic form throughout life, which gives it its iconic, almost mythical appearance. This extraordinary species is admired for its calm temperament, unique physiology, and remarkable ability to regenerate tissue.

Axolotls are special because they combine biological rarity with gentle character. Their regenerative powers are among the most studied in the animal kingdom, allowing them to regrow parts of their brain, heart, and limbs. They also remain fully aquatic throughout adulthood, which makes them especially fascinating to keepers and researchers alike. At Summer Axolotl Home, we celebrate them for both their scientific importance and their calming presence.
